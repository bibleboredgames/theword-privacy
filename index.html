Privacy Policy for theWord: Daily Bible Puzzle

Effective date: September 11, 2026

theWord ("the App") is developed by Luke Hess ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts, sign-ups, or personal information. You can play theWord without providing any personal details.

Game data stored on your device. Your puzzle progress, streak count, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see this data, and it is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Advertising data. The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for hints). AdMob may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general location (country/region) to serve and measure ads. On iOS, the App asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can learn how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalization at https://adssettings.google.com.

Children's Privacy

theWord is a family-friendly app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured with Google's content rating controls appropriate for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties except the service providers named above, which process data as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain no personal data on our own servers, because we don't operate any. Ad providers retain data according to their published policies.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, such requests are best directed to Google (links above), but you may always contact us for help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
