theWord Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for theWord: Daily Bible Puzzle

Effective date: October 18, 2026

theWord ("the App") is developed by Luke Hess ("we," "us"), who is responsible for this App. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. Questions can be sent to hello@bibleboredgames.com.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts, sign-ups, or personal information. You can play theWord without providing any personal details.

Game data stored on your device. Your puzzle progress, today's guesses, streak count, and hint use are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see this data, and it is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Advertising data. The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for hints). AdMob may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general location (country/region) to serve and measure ads. On iOS, the App asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can learn how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalization at https://adssettings.google.com.

No analytics. The App does not use any analytics service of its own.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, the App shows Google's consent message before ads are personalized or information is stored on or read from your device for advertising. You can choose Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options.

Legal basis. We rely on your consent for personalized advertising and for storing or reading information on your device for advertising. Where you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads and process limited data for purposes such as fraud prevention and ad measurement, as described in Google's policies. Your game data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Changing your choice. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by emailing us, and on iOS you can turn off tracking for the App in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Deleting and reinstalling the App also resets your choice.

International transfers. Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you may always contact us for help. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

theWord is a family-friendly app intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content, and sensitive ad categories are blocked. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties except the advertising provider named above, which processes data as described in its own privacy policy.

Data Retention

We retain no personal data on our own servers, because we don't operate any. Google retains advertising data according to its published policies.

Do Not Track

The App does not respond to web browser "Do Not Track" signals. On iOS, your App Tracking Transparency choice controls whether the App may track you.

Your Rights (other regions)

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, such requests are best directed to Google (links above), but you may always contact us for help.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
