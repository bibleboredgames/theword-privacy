Common questions

When does the new word appear?
A new Word of the Day starts at midnight, your phone's local time.

Why can't I play today's word again?
Like other daily puzzles, there's one word a day. When you come back, you'll see your finished board and the verse. For more practice, try Play Past Words from the main menu.

How does my streak work?
Solve the Word of the Day on consecutive days to grow your streak. Missing a day, or not solving the puzzle, starts it again. Your streak is stored on your phone, so deleting the App resets it.

How do hints work?
You get one hint per puzzle. It reveals one letter of the word. A short optional ad may play first; you get the hint either way.

Do I need an account?
No. theWord has no accounts or sign-ups.
